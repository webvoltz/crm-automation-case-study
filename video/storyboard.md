# Storyboard: product film

1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 89 s. One continuous 3D world, travelled by a single camera from left to right: clinic desk → gate corridor → engine room → backend and GoHighLevel → business map → sales pipeline → overview. Brand palette: charcoal and black, warm amber `#FFC107` for the active path, green for "passed", red for "stopped", blue for GoHighLevel and its signals. Every person, business and id on screen is made up.

| Time | Scene | Camera and action | On-screen text | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 – 0:11 | **1 · The problem** | Slow push-in on a clinic front desk: laptop, plant, warm wall light. A customer record card floats up, then a messaging workflow, a CRM dashboard, a reminder that's overdue, a duplicate record and a failed send. They drift apart and tilt away from each other. | "A simple action. A complex process." | Low ambient drone |
| 0:11 – 0:20 | **2 · The solution** | The cards dissolve. The camera moves onto the laptop screen: a review-requests list. The cursor clicks Approve on Maya Chen and the row turns green. An amber campaign cube rises out of the screen and the camera follows it right. | "One approval. A controlled workflow." | Soft click on Approve |
| 0:20 – 0:32 | **3 · Gatekeeping** | A corridor of seven gates, each with a short label: 2 h grace, 9 AM – 7 PM, contact sync, SMS opt-out, already reviewed?, frequency, re-check. The main cube passes and each lane light turns green. In a side lane, a cube waits at the send-window gate ("After 7 PM → waits for 9 AM"). In another, a cube is stopped at the opt-out gate and drops ("Opted out → stopped, no text"). The cube exits onto a green pad: "All checks passed". | "Every request is checked before it proceeds." | Quiet tick at each gate, a chime at the exit |
| 0:32 – 0:45 | **4 · Under the hood** | Wide shot of the engine room: the API block, PostgreSQL as an illuminated rack of rows, and a rail of queued jobs. Two worker drones arrive; one claims the first job and the other skips it ("locked → skipped") and takes the next. The claimed job turns red: "CRM timeout → job kept, retry in 30 s". A timer ring appears, the job is retried, turns green and carries on. | "Persistent state. Controlled retries. Safer processing." | Soft glass tick on the failure |
| 0:45 – 0:58 | **5 · GoHighLevel loop** | Two towers joined by two beams: our backend (amber, "rules, timing, duplicates") and GoHighLevel (blue, "messaging workflow"). The cube crosses the upper beam. A workflow panel lights step by step: tag added → contact updated → Text 1 sent → wait 2 days → reminder, if no review. Blue signals travel back along the lower beam: Text 1 sent, Reply, Review link clicked. A red duplicate hits a shield and falls away ("Duplicate → ignored"), then "Late event → state unchanged". | "Trigger. Track. Reconcile." | Music carries |
| 0:58 – 1:11 | **6 · Prospects to sales** | A dark city-block map; five amber pins rise over businesses. The Business Search panel shows five selected businesses, then the pipeline (Local outreach) and stage (New lead). Cards lift off the map and fly into the pipeline columns, each labelled with its result: existing contact reused, new contact created, uncertain match → human review, already in pipeline → skipped. | "From business discovery to an organized sales pipeline." | Music carries |
| 1:11 – 1:24 | **7 · The outcome** | The camera rises over the whole world: every zone, from the clinic to the pipeline, in one shot. A summary list builds on the left. On "connected", an amber line lights along the whole stage. | "Clear, connected, and traceable." Then the five-point summary | Music resolves |
| 1:24 – 1:29 | **End card** | Fade to black. The Webvoltz lockup settles in with a soft amber glow. | "Engineering reliable business automation." · webvoltz.com | Low impact on the logo |

Captions run under the whole film (hidden on the end card). Every animation is timed to the narration's word timings, so labels arrive when they're spoken.
